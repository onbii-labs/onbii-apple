# Governance

The Onbii project is stewarded and maintained by Yepyr B.V.

Yepyr B.V. manages the official repositories, releases, specification
publication, and commercial application distributions.

Contributions are accepted under the licence stated by the repository in which
they are submitted. Project stewardship does not alter the rights granted by
those licences.

The Onbii name and associated visual identity are not granted under the source
or specification licences except where explicitly stated.
